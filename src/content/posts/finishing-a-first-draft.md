The hardest part of a first draft isn't the beginning. It's the long middle, where the excitement fades and the plot starts asking questions you haven't answered.

## What helps me

- **A daily minimum.** A small, boring target beats waiting for inspiration.
- **Stopping mid-scene.** Ending a session when I know what comes next makes starting the next one easier.
- **A "fix later" list.** When I spot a problem in chapter 3 while writing chapter 12, I note it and keep moving.

## What I'm still figuring out

Pacing. I know it when I read it back, but I can't yet feel it while drafting.
