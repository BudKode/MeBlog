I'm Tanner. By day I'm a data engineer working in Python and SQL on Databricks. In my spare time I design parts in CAD and print them, and I'm working toward becoming a published author.

This site is where I write about all of it:

- **Data engineering**: pipelines, SQL patterns, and lessons from production
- **CAD and 3D printing**: designs, tolerances, and what the failed prints taught me
- **Writing**: craft notes and progress on my fiction

New interests will get their own topics as they come along.
