Welcome! If you've made it to this pathetic little blog, you either know me personally or a lot has changed in my life.

This is the place where all the scattered thoughts that make up my writing aspirations find an organized home.

I write because I want to create. One of the core aspects of my worldly ambition is to build something worth building.

I was in elementary school and was picking at pretty much everything that I rested my hands on. I don't know why. I do have a restless energy when I am bored I guess. I was pretty destructive though. If any paint or any kind of plastic covering was peeling up, I finished the job. Even relatively new material wasn't safe if I sat there long enough. I discovered all the ways to rub or roll something until the outer layer had enough of a bubble or a grip on it that I could pull off a piece. After that the rest came off easily and usually in satisfying chunks. My parents were involved in all kinds of events so I was sitting for extended periods pretty regularly as a kid and I left my mark wherever sat. My mom noticed. She was always very attentive to the small behaviors that could turn into larger aspects of my personality if left unchecked.

Her correction was, as usual, ill-received. I never publicly pouted or threw a fit. I'd learned that lesson a long time ago. Internally though, I'd stew. I'd reject what I was being told and do the bare minimum to show penitence but find a way to be stubborn in what I thought were unnoticeable ways. I don't remember how long this went on. Not more than a few weeks I think, but what did finally sink in for me was a comment about being destructive versus creative. I don't remember exact words, I rarely do, but the concept was that I could do with any actions in my life things that were destructive, or creative. She appealed to my ego and then elevated it to altruism. She reminded me that I had good ideas and could make things (honestly I think it had something to do with my good coloring) and do with my life something that would lift the lives of those around me and those who came after me, or I could be a force of entropy, wearing down the world around me faster than it naturally would, faster than life could keep up with.

Again, those were definitely not the words, but that's how I took it and that's what has stuck with me since.

You might think that is why I write, and it is, but it is also why I don't write. Writing is one aspect of my personality and one that I have consciously decided to dedicate only a small portion of my life to. Most of my life has been dedicated to building and supporting my own little family. That is a creative effort that I believe, for various reasons, will have the most enduring effect on the world around me and on those who come after me and I want to do it right.

The life I live doing that is fulfilling, but living with a nagging sense of creativity also causes me to invent stories from the smallest of little things. Again, I get bored. My hands have learned to fidget less, a little, but now my mind fidgets instead. You will probably notice that in what I write. I will focus on details that probably don't make sense to obsess over. Hopefully I will have an agent and an editor at some point that will help me dial that down, or dial it in if it works. That is probably the only thing that I can promise you about what you will find in my writing though. Stories that take the little what ifs in life and spin them into ideas and lives and worlds that I want to bring into being. They will be scattered across many genres and archetypes, but I hope to build them into enduring, appealing, and elevating works of art that lift me as I organize them, but also lift those around me.

I like atmosphere, slow-burning mysteries, sci-fi, and fantasy. Expect to see those too I guess, but if you are here reading this, I hope you will join me. Join me in exploring these little ideas that turn into worlds, and join me in building this world in creative ways.
