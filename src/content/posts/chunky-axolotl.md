## The VERY FIRST MODEL

I got the 3D printer for me. I don't want to pretend it was all about my kids, and that in any way this was not a selfish purchase, but I did look forward to making stuff for and with them. This was the very first thing we did together. I think axolotl's were already kind of old news by the time we made it but I am proud of my kids and the way that this turned out. It turned out decent for not knowing how to increase the triangle count on a tinkerCAD circle and one tutorial video. Someday I hope to revisit it and do it really really really well, but I'll keep this model up forever I think.
