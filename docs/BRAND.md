# MeBlog brand and content rules

Maintained by VP - Brand. Changes go through PRs; Tanner merges.

- MeBlog is the parent brand for Tanner's personal and hobby work. Software products and the Cody McReanor pen name are separate brands and do not mix with it.
- Fiction is published only under the pen name Cody McReanor, on Cody's page, never on the main blog.
- Jackie McReanor's page belongs to Lauren Cranor. She edits her own content and owns its copyright. Her posts carry "© Lauren Cranor, writing as Jackie McReanor."
- Maker projects appear on the main blog. Each MakerWorld design gets a page that links to its model.
- Only in-progress projects and the Jet Trooper mod show progress bars.
- Tanner writes the "making of" sections himself.
- Placeholder posts stay flagged until Tanner replaces them.
