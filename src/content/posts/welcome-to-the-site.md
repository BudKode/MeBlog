Hi, I'm glad you're here. This is my corner of the internet for the things I can't stop thinking about.

By day I'm a data engineer working in Python and SQL on Databricks. In the evenings I'm usually in a CAD program designing something to 3D print, or working on fiction. This site is where those threads meet.

## What you'll find here

- **Data engineering**: pipelines, SQL patterns, and lessons from production.
- **CAD and 3D printing**: designs, failed prints, and what I learned from them.
- **Writing**: craft notes and progress on my fiction.
- **Everything else**: new interests get a new tag, not a new website.

## A note on the pen name

My fiction appears under the name **Cody McCrainor**, which has its own section of this site. If you're here for stories, head over there.

Thanks for reading.
